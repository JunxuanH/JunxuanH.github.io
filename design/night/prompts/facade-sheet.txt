A 2x2 grid of four different seamless, tileable cyberpunk skyscraper façade textures. Each tile is a flat, straight-on orthographic close-up of a building face at night: near-black metal and concrete panels, rows of small rectangular windows (a few lit warm sodium-orange, a few lit cyan, most dark), thin glowing LED light strips in cyan or magenta running along panel seams, ventilation grilles, pipes, cable trays, rivets, small warning decals. No perspective, no sky, no ground, no people, uniform flat lighting, no text, no logos, no watermark. Each tile must tile seamlessly left-to-right and top-to-bottom. Thin black gutters between the four tiles.

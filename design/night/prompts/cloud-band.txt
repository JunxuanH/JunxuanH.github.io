A wide panoramic band of night cloud, painted in the same illustrated cyberpunk style as a matte skyline plate: layered wisps and soft cumulus banks lit from below by a city that is out of frame, dusty violet and mauve in the mass, warm sodium orange and faint magenta along the undersides, cool blue-grey in the thin upper tendrils.

The clouds sit on a pure black background. Black between and behind the banks, black at the top and the bottom of the strip, so the shapes can be cut out by their own brightness. Roughly half the strip is open black sky and half is cloud, with the banks strung out horizontally across the whole width in a broken, uneven line — thick masses, thin veils, and clear gaps.

No horizon, no ground, no buildings, no towers, no aircraft, no moon, no stars, no rain, no lettering, no frame or border, no vignette. Nothing but cloud on black, evenly exposed from edge to edge, soft painterly edges rather than photographic detail.
